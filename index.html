    • ABOUT HECTOR MONROY

    Hector Monroy "Monrock". Mexican master of pop art style, born in 1953 in Queretaro, Mexico. Studied advertising as well as artistic drawing in the late 70s, and began working in the audiovisual industry as a graphic designer. For more than 40 years, Monroy has created original oil paintings inspired by iconic personalities such as The Beatles, Marilyn Monroe, Elvis Presley, Muhammad Ali and many others. His artwork is part of private collections throughout Mexico and the United States.

    ORIGINAL ARTWORK

    Every artwork in the Monrock Collection is an original creation by Hector Monroy. Each piece is unique and includes a hand-signed Certificate of Authenticity. Monrock paintings are created using professional-grade materials, including Winsor & Newton Winton oil colors, ensuring lasting quality and vibrant color.
