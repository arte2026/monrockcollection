Pop Artist Hector Monroy


Welcome to the official website of artist Hector Monroy. For many years, Hector has painted the greatest legends of rock music, cinema, and sports, starting with his own personal idols. His work blends pop art with realistic portraiture, creating high-contrast, richly saturated pieces with internationally recognized, photographic detail—a signature aesthetic known as the "Monrock Pop Art Style." Based in Mexico, Hector has produced an extensive body of work celebrating iconic cultural figures. He is one of Mexico's most successful crossover artists, with more than 300 pieces sold to private collectors and fans across the United States. “My greatest satisfaction is connecting with people who appreciate my work enough to hang a piece on their wall.” With an established career and a bold creative vision, Hector Monroy continues to bring a fresh, dynamic energy to the global pop art movement.

“My passion, my hobby, my job, my idols, my muse, my collection, my life, everything!”

About Hector Monroy

Hector Monroy ''Monrock''. Mexican master of pop art style, born in 1953 in Queretaro, Mexico. Studied advertising as well as artistic drawing in the late 70s, and began working in the audiovisual communications industry as a graphic designer. At the age of seventeen made his first drawing in pop art of The Beatles. "My inspiration is drawn from rock music and art; I pay tribute to the most notable icons of music and Hollywood golden years". With more than 40 years working in art business, his art portraits of Icons, Myths and Legends of Rock Music and Film, achieve to capture all the detail and personality of those nostalgic wonderful decades of the 50's, 60's and 70`s like: Marilyn Monroe, The Beatles, Pink Floyd, Led Zeppelin, The Who, The Doors, James Dean, Elvis Presley, John Wayne, Bettie Page, Madonna, Muhammad Ali and more.
